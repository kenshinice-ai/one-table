From "what should we cook?" to footsteps through your centre

A complete reunion-dinner campaign for your centre: an interactive bilingual menu planner as the centrepiece, surrounded by the campaign creative to carry it — produced end to end by one Melbourne team.

The gap we close

Menu-planning kiosks exist in Australian centres — they answer "what should we cook tonight?" and hand the shopper a recipe QR. The journey ends exactly where your tenants' revenue should begin. Nobody shows the shopper where inside your centre to buy the 18 ingredients on that list, or which of your restaurants serves the same table.

One Table: the menu becomes a route

  1. Scan a QR — poster, kiosk or centre app. No install.
  2. Guests, budget, courses, dietary needs → a complete menu, instantly.
  3. Every ingredient merges into one shopping list, scaled to the party.
  4. The list becomes a walking route — each ingredient mapped to a tenant, ordered level by level, saved to the phone. Works offline on basement levels.
  5. Rather book a table? The same menu routes to your restaurants.

Built the trustworthy way

  • Curated, editorially reviewed recipes — no on-the-fly AI text under your brand
  • Allergen exclusions are rule-based and absolute: contains, derived-from, may-contain, unknown — all blocked
  • Fully bilingual English / 中文 across menus, lists and route cards
  • No accounts, no personal data — privacy by design, aggregate campaign metrics only
Every route is a multi-tenant journey — measured in the campaign report, not hoped for.
