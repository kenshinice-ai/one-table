## 1. Services（服务范围）

1.1 Provider will supply the "One Table" white-label menu-planning platform configured for the Client's venue, comprising: (a) branded web application at an agreed subdomain; (b) venue location mapping (ingredient-to-tenant/aisle) up to [N] points of interest; (c) route-card generation; (d) launch assets as itemised in Schedule A.
1.2 Where the engagement is a **Campaign Package**, Schedule A additionally itemises creative deliverables (key visual, posters, social clips, photography) with quantities and formats.
1.3 Anything not itemised in Schedule A is out of scope; changes via §9.

## 2. Term & Renewal

2.1 Campaign engagements: fixed term [start–end], no auto-renewal.
2.2 Annual licences: 12 months, renewing unless either party gives 30 days' notice. ⚖️ *auto-renewal notice requirements for business contracts*

## 3. Fees & Payment

3.1 Setup fee: 50% on signing, 50% on go-live. Recurring fees monthly/annually in advance, 14-day terms.
3.2 Late payment: suspension rights after [14] days' written notice. ⚖️
3.3 Fees exclude GST; GST itemised on invoices.

## 4. Client Obligations

4.1 Client supplies: brand assets with authority to license them; venue floor-plan reference material; tenant directory; timely approvals ([5] business days, deemed approval after reminder ⚖️).
4.2 Client warrants it has rights in all supplied material; indemnifies Provider for third-party IP claims arising from supplied material. ⚖️ *indemnity scope*

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 Platform, recipe database, editorial content, illustrations and route-card designs remain Provider's property. Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence for the Term, limited to the venue and agreed channels. **No sublicensing.**
5.2 Venue floor-plan *schematics* are redrawn by Provider from Client reference material; the schematic (as a derived artistic work) vests in Provider, licensed to Client for the Term. ⚖️ *derived-work ownership wording*
5.3 Each party's trade marks remain its own; co-branding per Schedule B (dual-logo lockup mandatory on guest-facing surfaces).
5.4 Case study rights: Provider may reference the engagement (venue name, campaign visuals, aggregate metrics) in its portfolio [if founding-rate applies: mandatory; otherwise: with approval not unreasonably withheld]. ⚖️

## 6. Data & Privacy

6.1 The platform operates without user accounts and collects **no personal information** from guests. Aggregate, anonymous event counts only (scans, menus composed, lists exported, route cards saved) are collected and reported to Client.
6.2 Neither party will introduce tracking that changes 6.1 without written agreement. ⚖️ *Privacy Act applicability statement*

## 7. Disclaimers & Liability

7.1 Content disclaimers as per Schedule C (the DISCLAIMERS pack: planning estimates, allergen verification, indicative routing, third-party information). Client must not remove or obscure them.
7.2 ⚖️ **Liability clause for lawyer to draft**: cap at fees paid in prior 12 months; carve-outs; **must not purport to exclude non-excludable ACL consumer guarantees** — needs the standard ACL-compliant wording.
7.3 Provider holds Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance of not less than [$X]m / [$Y]m. ⚖️ *typical venue-required minimums*

## 8. Service Levels（年度 license 适用）

8.1 Platform availability target 99.5% monthly (excluding scheduled maintenance, notified 48h ahead).
8.2 Venue-mapping changes (tenant moved/closed): updated within [5] business days of written notice.
8.3 Seasonal content refresh: quarterly, per Schedule A.

## 9. Change Control / 10. Termination / 11. Post-termination

9.1 Changes by written variation with quoted fees.
10.1 Either party for material breach unremedied in [14] days; Provider may suspend for non-payment (§3.2).
11.1 On expiry/termination: guest-facing deployment taken offline within [7] days; Client licence ends; Provider retains platform and content; final aggregate report delivered. ⚖️ *refund treatment on early termination*

Governing law Victoria; notices; assignment; entire agreement; force majeure; dispute resolution ladder (negotiation → mediation → courts of Victoria).
